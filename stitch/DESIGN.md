## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-end botanical sanctuary in digital space. Built around clean beauty, natural nourishment, and skin soothing science, it combines minimal editorial luxury with delicate organic glassmorphism. The aesthetic evokes the serene, cooling touch of morning dew, sliced fresh cucumber, and pure aloe vera gel. 

Key attributes include:
- **Atmospheric Calm:** Expansive, breathing layouts that avoid density or urgency, prioritizing gentle pacing and generous whitespace.
- **Editorial Organic Luxury:** High-fashion, bespoke apothecary presentation blending refined typography with modern botanical skincare cues.
- **Tactile Purity:** Semi-translucent frosted glass containers, whisper-thin borders, and smooth rounded pill shapes that mirror clean glass skincare packaging and water droplets.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in botanical tones drawn directly from fresh aloe flesh, cooling cucumber water, and deep forest apothecary glass.

- **Primary (`#2E5A44`):** Deep Aloe Leaf. Used for high-emphasis interactive elements, core brand headlines, key actions, and grounding structural accents.
- **Secondary (`#C7E2D0`):** Cucumber Tint / Soft Dew. Serves as a soothing accent for badges, soft chip backgrounds, interactive hover states, and organic highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#1B382B`):** Forest Sage Noir. A deep botanical green-black delivering crisp readability for text, high-contrast labels, and premium structural borders.
- **Neutral Backgrounds (`#FAF9F5` base, `#F4F2EB` surface):** Warm linen and off-white silk tones that replace harsh clinical white with comforting, radiant warmth.
- **Tint Highlight (`#E8F2EA`):** Ultra-light botanical mist for table rows, secondary surface containers, and frosted glass cards.

## Typography

The typography pairing contrasts the artisanal editorial elegance of Playfair Display with the clean, highly legible geometry of Plus Jakarta Sans.

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Conveys the prestige and tradition of natural apothecary skincare. Headline levels utilize relaxed, open line-heights to ensure titles feel unhurried and editorial.
- **Body & Captions (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides modern clinical clarity, ensuring formula percentages, ingredients, and routine instructions remain crisp and readable at all screen densities.
- **Micro-Copy & Eyebrow Badges:** Formatted in uppercase `label-md` or `label-sm` with expanded tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to mimic luxury cosmetic packaging stamps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an airy 12-column grid on desktop and a flexible 4-column grid on mobile devices. Generous outer margins prevent product imagery and botanical copy from feeling crowded.

- **Desktop (min-width: 1024px):** Max container width capped at `1280px` centered within a `#FAF9F5` canvas, utilizing `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Fluid 8-column layout with `2rem` margin and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding. Component vertical rhythms rely on `space-lg` and `space-xl` between sections to preserve an unhurried, serene reading pace.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system eschews heavy drop shadows in favor of botanical glassmorphism, soft ambient occlusion, and delicate hairline boundaries:

- **Surface Glassmorphism:** Card containers use `background: rgba(250, 249, 245, 0.72)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and an inner border `rgba(46, 90, 68, 0.08)` to replicate frosted cosmetic jars.
- **Ambient Botanical Shadow:** Floating elements (modals, drawers, interactive dropdowns) cast a dual-layer, diffuse shadow tinted with deep sage: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.03)`.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines:** Outlines and dividers are rendered with hairline 1px strokes in `#E3EDE5` or `rgba(46, 90, 68, 0.12)`, preserving airiness without creating rigid partitions.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates rounded, pebble-inspired geometry (level 3) to reflect the smooth texture of aloe gel and botanical river stones:

- **Buttons & Chips:** Full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / `9999px`) for a soft, touchable tactile presence.
- **Cards & Containers:** `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem to 2rem) for product showcases and glass panels.
- **Form Inputs:** Soft oval capsules or deep `rounded-xl` borders to maintain continuity with cosmetic packaging silhouettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), filled with `#2E5A44`, white text, tracking `0.06em`. Hover transitions gently to `#1B382B` with a subtle `transform: translateY(-1px)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a 1px border in `#2E5A44`, text in `#2E5A44`. Hover reveals a whisper of cucumber tint (`rgba(199, 226, 208, 0.3)`).
- **Glass Floating Action:** Semi-translucent frosted pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` with blur) trimmed in `#C7E2D0`.

### Chips & Pill Badges
- **Ingredient & Benefit Tags (e.g., "Organic Aloe", "99% Pure Hydration"):** Soft pill shape, background `#E8F2EA`, text `#2E5A44`, font style `label-sm` uppercase.

### Cards
- **Product & Formula Showcase Cards:** Frosted cream surface (`#FAF9F5` at 85% opacity, backdrop blur 12px), bordered with 1px `rgba(46, 90, 68, 0.1)`. Generous internal padding (`space-lg`), housing centered editorial imagery, botanical ingredient callouts, and smooth pricing triggers.

### Input Fields
- **Text Inputs & Quantity Selectors:** Oval pill styling or `rounded-xl` containers, background `#F4F2EB`, border 1px solid `rgba(46, 90, 68, 0.16)`. Focus state shifts the border to `#2E5A44` with a 3px soft outer ring in `rgba(199, 226, 208, 0.4)`. Placeholder text in muted botanical grey-green (`rgba(27, 56, 43, 0.5)`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded squares (6px radius), border in `#2E5A44`. Checked state fills with `#2E5A44` showcasing an off-white checkmark.
- **Radios:** Circular indicators with a concentric inner dot in `#2E5A44` against a clean `#FAF9F5` field.

### Botanical Accent Modifiers
- **Accordion Panels (Ritual & Ingredients):** Minimalist horizontal line dividers with custom thin plus/minus botanical toggles, smooth vertical ease expansions, and quiet typography.
