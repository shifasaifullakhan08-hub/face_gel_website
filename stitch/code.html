Answers & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it suitable for acne-prone or congested skin?

expand_more

Yes, absolutely. The formula is 100% non-comedogenic, oil-free, and silicone-free. Aloe vera contains naturally occurring salicylic compounds that gently clarify pores while cucumber hydrosol and niacinamide soothe post-breakout irritation without clogging pores.

Can this replace my heavy winter moisturiser?

expand_more

For oily and combination skin, it provides complete standalone hydration year-round. If you experience extreme dry winter weather, you can layer this hydrating gel directly beneath a richer lipid cream or face oil to lock in multi-depth moisture.

Does it cause piling when wearing sunscreen or foundation?

expand_more

No. Unlike gels formulated with synthetic thickeners like carbomers or heavy silicones, our fluid botanical water-burst matrix absorbs completely into the epidermis in 15 seconds, making it an ideal pre-makeup primer.

What is the shelf life and storage recommendation?

expand_more

Unopened jars retain optimum botanical efficacy for 24 months. Once opened, we suggest enjoying within 6 months. Storing your jar in a cool place or beauty fridge extends potency and provides enhanced soothing relief.
